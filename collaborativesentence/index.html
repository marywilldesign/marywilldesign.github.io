Longest Collaborative Sentence – Remixed

UX graphic design web dev personal project

2025

This project considered how we obscure and censor text, especially when people are left to their own devices. Using web scraping and conditional programming, remixed this art piece originally intended to be a collaborative piece. The toughest part of the project was not pushing my developing boundaries but having to sit through a large chunk of it to find words which could be deemed problematic – including nuances.

So far, I have permission from the Whitney Museum of American Art but not yet from the Douglas Davis estate so, until then, this website will just be on my local server. I hope to share it one day as I believe it takes a few hours to sit through the whole thing, given my webscraping script drew in around 160+ text files!
