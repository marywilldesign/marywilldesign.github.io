Paper Cut poster folio

graphic design personal project

Inspired by a facsimile of Matisse's Jazz Manifesto found at a Milan flea market, I created a paper-based poster series exploring Acid House music. Using coloured paper, high-resolution scans, and photography, I translated the energy of the music into layered, tactile compositions.
