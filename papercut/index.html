Acid House Manifesto

... Let me explain. I was travelling in Milan and, when I ventured out to a flea market, came across Matisse's Jazz Manifesto, one of the key reasons I was motivated to join the design industry. After a long arduous journey (involving some hefty bartering), I came home with this gigantic facsimile of this seminal work.

This inspired me to create a work of my own, using similar methods. At the time, I was very deep into Acid House (think A Guy Called Gerald etc.). So, equipped with coloured paper, a love for high DPI scans, and a good camera, I got to work on visualizing how this music feels.
