2024 · role: Graphic Designer

Acid House Manifesto

During a trip to Milan, I bought a facsimile Matisse's Jazz Manifesto at a flea market. Inspired by its vibrant, tactile approach to visual storytelling, I decided to create my own paper-based work in a similar spirit. Drawing on my interest in Acid House music, I used coloured paper, high-resolution scans, and photography to translate the energy and rhythm of the music into a visual format. The result is a layered, dynamic poster series that explores the intersection of music, movement, and design.
