All / Live Open Call: Installation

Live Open Call @ Torpedo Books

An open call was sent out in the form of posters, posts, and invitations across UK, Canada, and Norway, ahead of an opening night. The submissions were printed in one long receipt and made into a website (art-kive).


To compare the difference between a premeditated open call and a live one, I designed and built an interface which enabled visitors to scan a QR code and send text and images to a receipt printer, bridging digital and physical interactions. These digital submissions left an immediate tangible trace in the physical exhibition space, rather than disappearing into a screen or database.

Highlight/s

A countdown was added to the submission site allowing promotion ahead of the opening night. Submissions were sent in remotely from Canada and Australia, when the opening night was in Oslo, Norway. This meant that there was a continuous stream of submissions coming in while the site was up.

A live thermal receipt printer was selected as the output instead of a screen display or regular printer, meaning the continuous nature of the output linked each submission in some form. There were also custom design elements in the receipt output, like typeface selection and image format.

Outcome

Had some great feedback from the opening night and managed to gather over 100 submissions between the open calls. The installation went ahead with no technical glitches and it became a piece that I would want to run again as it was another way of getting visitors to co-create.

Contribution

  • Established a backend to allow a QR-based submission flow for text and images, integrating the browser-based submission to the printer
  • Made a simple interface design for visitor input which connected to their camera and allowed a (limited character) text submission
  • Scanned both receipts and uploaded them to the site, allowing people's submissions to be permanently archived
