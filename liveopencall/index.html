Live Open Call

graphic design web dev print

Designed and built an interface for gallery visitors to scan a QR code and send text + images to a live receipt printer, bridging digital and physical.

2025 · Designer and Developer

view site ↗

ASK

Asked to create a piece inspired by Mail Art. Explored the idea of open calls and decided on a live, participatory interface connecting visitor phones to a physical receipt printer in the gallery space. Here, co-creation was at the forefront of interaction.

PROBLEM

Needed a low-friction way for gallery visitors with no prior context to participate spontaneously, without an app download or account.

OPPORTUNITY

How might we make physical/digital participation as frictionless as scanning a code, while keeping the output feel intentional rather than gimmicky?

RESULT

Visitors used it throughout the exhibition run, with roughly 65 submissions continually running all night with a final output that changed and morphed with the evening. It helped to gather people and the Mail Art founder of Norway, Guttorm Nordø, personally came up and congratulated me on the piece.

CONTRIBUTION

  • Designed and built the QR-triggered submission interface and receipt-printer output pipeline
  • Designed the visual/typographic treatment for printed output
  • Handled technical integration between web form and physical printer hardware
