Subtext film poster

graphic design print

Poster design and branding for an award-winning Canadian short film, translating narrative subtext into a single visual frame.

2026 · Graphic Designer

view on imdb ↗

CONCEPT

The film's central theme was around communication in intimate relationships. The poster needed to hint at the film's subtext without giving away plot.

PROCESS

Explored a number of different directions before landing on the final composition. Worked from stills and explored typography options before landing on what the film team had decided on for the credits for consistency.

REFLECTION

It was an interesting transition, working on visual storytelling but for a single frame without revealing the plot or giving too much away. The poster has represented the film, which has been nominated for a number of awards.
