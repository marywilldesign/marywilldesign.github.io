Speleo Record Label

graphic design

2025 · role: Graphic Designer

I always enjoy working with someone from my network. I was brought on to come up with ideas for a record label that somehow incorporated a vinyl record but was experimental and “zesty”.

At some point we explored the etymology of speleo. The prefix comes from Ancient Greek σπήλαιον (spḗlaion), meaning "cave", which led to typographic experiments.
