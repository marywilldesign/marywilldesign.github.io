Speleo Record Label

graphic design

2025

I always enjoy working with someone from my network. I was brought on to come up with some ideas for a record label idea which somehow encorporated a vinyl record but was 'zesty' and experimental. Drawing inspiration from other labels, I tried to push the idea in a few different directions, giving my client an idea of the possibilities.

At some point during the engagement, we started talking about the etymology of 'speleo. A quick Google: the prefix "speleo-" comes from the Ancient Greek word σπήλαιον (spḗlaion), meaning "cave". This led to some type experiements.
