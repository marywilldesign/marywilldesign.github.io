Speleo Record Label

graphic design

I was brought on to develop a visual identity for an independent Vancouver record label. The brief asked for something that incorporated a vinyl record while remaining experimental and energetic. Through exploration of the name's etymology (from Ancient Greek for "cave"), I developed a flexible brand system based on typographic mark-making across digital and print touchpoints.
