Glyphs: Corrupted

graphic design print personal project

Three experimental typefaces exploring the boundaries of legibility by corrupting letterforms through systematic deformation. Using scraping and generative manipulation, the project examines how text breaks down under transformation, shifting between readable type and abstract visual form.
