Modo Carsharing B2C

UX graphic design

End-to-end website overhaul for Vancouver's largest B-Corp carsharing coop, simplifying the content experience across marketing and member touchpoints.

2022 - ongoing · UX/UI Designer and Scrum Master

Project Management: Sarah Bain, Thelma Wiegert

User Research: Rebecca Harrington

full case study (pdf) →

ASK

Vancouver's largest B-Corp carsharing coop needed a complete website overhaul to simplify the content experience across marketing and member touchpoints, aligned with a broader brand and app refresh.

PROBLEM

The existing site had grown organically over time, resulting in duplicated content, unclear navigation paths, and a CMS that made it difficult for the marketing team to make updates independently.

OPPORTUNITY

How might we restructure the information architecture and redesign the site to serve both prospective members exploring carsharing and existing members managing their accounts, while giving the marketing team more autonomy?

RESULT

Delivered a fully re-platformed site with streamlined IA, refreshed brand-aligned design, and a CMS that empowered the marketing team to make updates without developer support.

CONTRIBUTION

  • Served as UX/UI Designer and Scrum Master across the full project lifecycle
  • Led competitive analysis, information architecture restructuring, and hi-fi prototyping
  • Collaborated across a cross-functional team of project managers, UI designers, researchers, content strategists, and developers
