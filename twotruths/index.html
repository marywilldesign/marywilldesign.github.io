Two Truths & AI game

UX graphic design web dev personal project

2025

In the theme of lying and having recently played “two truths and a lie”, I thought it would be a fun exercise to make a web-based experienced which harnessed AI to churn out statements, with one being a lie.

Home page where the eye is animated to blink and look around the page.

Roles: Ideation, UX design, Web Development, Graphic Design

The Challenge

I am lucky to be a confident frontend developer but, given my limited backend experience, from old portfolio and for-fun-sites, it was nice to have AI on my side. After lots of trial and error, asking a some dev friends and chatGPT, I landed on a neat little solution.

Snapshot of .json script

This allowed me to generate a large number of 'two truths' and one lie for each group en masse. The themes which I focused on ranged from Oslo-based art and Swiss design to fun facts about Australia and Fluxus design.

Objectives

  • Create a game which harnesses AI, where users need to select the lie from the list
  • The site itself must be simple and intuitive
  • Explore the idea of 'tricking' users, what is AI and what is not?
