Riso prints at Kunstnernes Hus

graphic design print exhibits

2025

In conjunction with the 2Truths&AI web game, I created a series of risograph posters exhibited at Kunstnernes Hus in Oslo as part of the There is no exhibit, it's not happening show. The concept explored authorship, truth, and AI-generated imagery — revealing that none of the posters were actually AI-made, despite appearances.
