Riso prints at Kunstnernes Hus

graphic design print exhibits

Riso print series exhibited at Kunstnernes Hus, Oslo, pushing colour, overprint, and layout experimentation through the limitations of the medium.

2025 · Graphic Designer

CONCEPT

I explored the idea of two truths and a lie in poster format, combining AI design aesthetics with traditional printing formats. The only lie, however, was that no AI was used, just fabricated through the text on the posters.

PROCESS

Riso printing forces you to design around its constraints: limited ink colours, registration imprecision, and layered passes. I did two posters with 3-colour passes and one with 2 colours. There were a number of happy accidents that occurred in the form of misprints: the filled-in hole on Mahjong and the white glitch on the red background on Rummikub.

MATERIALS / FORMAT

A3, Munken Pure Rough 150g

REFLECTION

Risograph is a form of analogue printing which I really appreciate. Its natural glitches and textured output add a sense of human touch to a piece.
