An exhibit about lying

In conjunction with the 2Truths&AI web game, I made a set of posters which were on display at Oslo's kunstnerneshus Gallery for the There is no exhibit, it's not happening exhibit. The idea was that out of the three posters, one of them was made by AI. Now that the exhibit is over, the truth is: none of them were.
